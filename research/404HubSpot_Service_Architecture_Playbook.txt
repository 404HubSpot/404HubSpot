What the research says
• Competitors sell outcomes with a price. Bonnie Computer Hub puts a "from KSh" figure on every one of its website
types and pairs it with a 3-month academy that ends in an internship. Visitors know the cost before they message.
• The market is wide, so bands matter. A basic Nairobi site can cost KSh 10,000 while complex e-commerce can pass
KSh 300,000. Without clear tiers, every enquiry becomes a custom negotiation.
• Nakuru is open ground. The local tech scene is described as emerging with no anchor hub like LakeHub or SwahiliBox,
and county ICT hubs focus on basics such as office software and online freelancing, not software engineering.
• Your edge is already in your meta description: M-Pesa integration, modern web and mobile builds, plus classes.
Competitors mostly do one of these. Few do all of them with a build-and-teach loop.

   Why the Discovery Sprint matters
   The most common pricing mistake is quoting before understanding the project, because "a website" can mean a hundred
   different things. A KSh 5,000 paid scoping step filters out time-wasters, produces a written fixed-scope quote and is credited
   back when the client proceeds.



Two ways the pillars feed each other
• Academy to Build: the best graduates join your delivery bench through the internship, which lowers your cost per project.
• Build to Academy: every client project becomes a case study and a capstone brief for students.

• Process: one-week discovery, design in Figma, two-week build sprints with a clickable build each time, store submission,
then post-launch fixes.
• Design for weak networks: test on mid-range Android phones over mobile data and keep key flows working offline where

   Keep it honest

   publish app prices as fixed. Publish "from" figures and a Discovery Sprint.

How to fill it in (30 minutes)
• Step 1: each person lists their top three skills and one thing they enjoy teaching.
• Step 2: match people to roles, leaving a role empty rather than forcing a fit. An empty role is a hiring or training plan.
• Step 3: set each person's monthly billable hours and a rate. For early-career developers, KES 500 - 1,000 per hour is a
common starting range, and agencies charge 2-5x that.
• Step 4: publish skill tags and the courses each person teaches on the team page, because competitors' team pages hide
this.


   Rule of thumb for scheduling
   No one should teach more than two tracks a quarter while carrying client work. If a role has only one person, plan a backup,
   because a sick day should not cancel a cohort or a launch.

   Check this first: can search engines read your pages?
   When I fetched the home page and the /services and /team addresses, I only received the page title and description, not the
   content. That means the site builds its pages in the browser. Visitors are fine, but crawlers, link previews and some tools may
   see an empty shell. Confirm in Google Search Console, and if it is the case, move to server-rendered or pre-rendered pages
   (for example Next.js) so services and prices are indexable.



Quick wins this week
• Add a WhatsApp button on every page with a pre-filled message per service.
• Publish the four website tiers and the three care plans on one pricing page.
• Replace long service lists with five pillar cards, each with a from-price.
• Put "M-Pesa integration" and "Learn to code in Nakuru" in the home page headline area.
• Write one guide post: "How much does a website cost in Kenya?" using your own tiers.
• Only announce tracks that have a start date.
